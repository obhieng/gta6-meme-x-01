## Patterns that win (what the judge rewards)

1. **Fake official document / real-UI format, not a captioned photo-meme.** 4 of the top 5 are bureaucratic documents (Steam receipt, library overdue notice, parking citation) or a phone UI (lock screen); #3 is a TV news/weather graphic. All mimic a real artifact with dense, legible small text. The top 20 is dominated by the same family: boarding pass (#7), bank statement (#9), receipt (#11, #13), tax refund (#14), DMV vision test (#15), birth certificate (#16), hospital discharge bill (#17). A fresh document type can still tie for #1 (the hurricane, #3).
2. **The 13-year wait, quantified to absurd specificity.** Every top-5 entry is built on "13 years, 2 delays" plus exact numbers: 4,811 days, 214 GB, 99%, 87 trailer rewatches, $481.10 vs $79.99, $4.12 balance. Specific numbers are the jokes — each itemised line is its own micro-gag ("family photos, deleted", "patience: NONE LEFT", "sick days: doctor approved"). Generic "finally GTA 6" without numbers does not appear at the top.
3. **make.fun is the punchline, not a watermark.** In all 5, make.fun appears as the thing that *paid* — "+$250.00 bounty payout", "PAID by my AI agent on make.fun" — resolving the joke's cost. The ghost logo appears in all 5 images, but always inside the fiction (payment method, stamp, hurricane eye, police badge, notification). The brand is the hero of the story, never a corner logo.
4. **In-world Leonida/Vice City framing with GTA lore.** "Leonida" appears in all 5 (Leonida Public Library, Leonida Weather Service, Leonida Police Dept, Leonida 5G); Jason & Lucia named in #2 and #5; Vice City in #2–#4. The meme lives inside GTA's world, not outside it commenting on it.
5. **Dense itemisation: 4–7 escalating micro-jokes per image.** Each artifact is a list where every line adds a gag, building to the make.fun payoff at the bottom. Square 512×512, high contrast, text still legible at thumbnail size — the judge sees the artifact, so readability at small size matters.

## Things to avoid

1. **A bare logo/watermark in the corner.** Winners integrate make.fun into the joke as the payer; a detached logo adds nothing and wastes the punchline slot. (Also: the #3 entry shows the logo can even *be* the visual gag — the hurricane's eye.)
2. **Vague waiting jokes with no numbers.** "Waited so long lol" with no dates, days, GB, dollars or counts is the exact thing the top 5 all avoid — every winner quantifies the wait (4,811 days, 214 GB, 99%, $481.10).
3. **Repeating the most crowded formats without a twist.** Receipts/invoices already saturate the top 20 (#1, #9, #11, #13, #18) and 14 entries are undefeated 12-0, so a plain receipt now fights a dozen near-identical ones on tiny score margins. A fresh document/UI conceit (like #3's hurricane warning or #5's lock screen) differentiates; a me-too receipt needs a sharper item list to stand out.
